# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Steam PC gamers with large libraries (hundreds of games) hit by backlog paralysis.
They know they own something worth playing but can't decide what. They open this
at a desk in the evening, looking for a trusted answer in under 30 seconds, not
another tool they have to manually curate.

## Product Purpose

Steam Atlas turns a raw Steam library into a decision engine: import once, get
taste signals, backlog insights, genre analytics, and a "play next" recommendation
that shows its reasoning. Success means the user finds a clear answer fast and
trusts it because the logic is visible.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and a little dry. A tool that knows its own output. Not playful,
not corporate. Speaks in conclusions, not suggestions.

## Anti-references

- Steam Store: promotional, cluttered, optimized for sales not clarity
- RGB gaming dashboards (Overwolf, esport-branded tools): neon on black, aggressive
- Generic SaaS hero grids: big numbers, gradient accent, card-icon-text repeated
- Any interface with decorative metric bars that carry no real data

## Design Principles

1. Explain the reasoning — every recommendation shows why; no decorative math
2. Calm command center — quiet authority, not visual spectacle
3. Data earns its display — if it's not real user data, it's not on screen
4. Performance is a feature — effects are cut if they cost GPU without value
5. Build on what works — the stack and token vocabulary stay; execution improves

## Accessibility and Inclusion

WCAG AA. Reduced-motion respected. No color-only status signaling.
